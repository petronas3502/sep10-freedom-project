## Part A: existing technology

##### Software
* MyChart
  * Easy access to your health information.
  * Notifies you on upcoming appointments and test results.
  * Provides summaries on appointments and information on any health concerns and any medications needed.
  * [MyChart](https://encrypted-tbn0.gstatic.com/images?q=tbn:ANd9GcSu0n2yqqqXDX8w1Cwk-nOt8fjPrBz5B2fGg282MSDgd1AWI7jNbSbYdo8&s=10)
* Teladoc
  * affordable medical care
  * options to prescribe medication
  * available in all languages
  * [Teladoc](https://encrypted-tbn0.gstatic.com/images?q=tbn:ANd9GcRmNCJHfIgN5QHtoeEh0vDO6-5bHNwn-KqwBgdQWZRuBVv7paX5qnkRpi8s&s=10)
* Medisafe
  * helps users manage chronic conditions by sending reminders to take medications
  * virtual pillbox notifications
  * tracks progress with daily and weekly reports
  * caregiver support
  * [Medisafe](https://encrypted-tbn0.gstatic.com/images?q=tbn:ANd9GcQl5XMqZOoOcgL4vZSuVdsTAcgyLi1Tc6AB9EhmTXmH9rPOA4JCd_fqSsbU&s=10)
##### Hardware
* electrocardiogram
  * test how the heart is beating
  * checks electrical signals in the heart
  * helps find or detect heart attacks and irregular heartbeats
  * helps with related problems in the body but more specifically the chest area where the heart is
  * link I used https://www.mayoclinic.org/tests-procedures/ekg/about/pac-20384983
  * [Electrocardiogram](https://encrypted-tbn0.gstatic.com/images?q=tbn:ANd9GcRYnkdtvJsarwMx-6_2HKt8deKayeD11sKOVoDU4QnqNQ&s)
