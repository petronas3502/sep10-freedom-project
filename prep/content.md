## Part A: existing technology

##### Software
* MyChart
  * Easy access to your health information.
  * Notifies you on upcoming appointments and test results.
  * Provides summaries on appointments and information on any health concerns and any medications needed.
  * [MyChart](https://encrypted-tbn0.gstatic.com/images?q=tbn:ANd9GcSu0n2yqqqXDX8w1Cwk-nOt8fjPrBz5B2fGg282MSDgd1AWI7jNbSbYdo8&s=10)
* Teladoc
  * affordable medical care
  * options to prescribe medication
  * available in all languages
  * [Teladoc](https://encrypted-tbn0.gstatic.com/images?q=tbn:ANd9GcRmNCJHfIgN5QHtoeEh0vDO6-5bHNwn-KqwBgdQWZRuBVv7paX5qnkRpi8s&s=10)

##### Hardware
* Electrocardiogram
  * test how the heart is beating
  * checks electrical signals in the heart
  * helps find or detect heart attacks and irregular heartbeats
  * helps with related problems in the body but more specifically the chest area where the heart is
  * link I used https://www.mayoclinic.org/tests-procedures/ekg/about/pac-20384983
  * [Electrocardiogram](https://encrypted-tbn0.gstatic.com/images?q=tbn:ANd9GcRYnkdtvJsarwMx-6_2HKt8deKayeD11sKOVoDU4QnqNQ&s)
* Nebulizer
  * turns liquid medicine into a mist that can be easily inhaled
  * used for people with lung diseases
  * comes in different sizes, a portable size you can take anywhere and a regular size you have to plug into a wall to use
  * different from an inhaler in size and feel
  * link I used https://medlineplus.gov/ency/patientinstructions/000006.htm
  * [Nebulizer](https://encrypted-tbn0.gstatic.com/images?q=tbn:ANd9GcSfaiipHvWqxUJDWqSWkBB8ts-pmwymd0gD8Me2GWTyEOMYBGx217aB8-I&s=10)
* Diathermy
  * used in electrosurgery, a technique often used in surgery to control bleeding and to rapidly dissect soft tissue in surgery
  * produces high frequency electrical current to cut, coagulate, dessicate, and fulgarate tissue
  * helps in preventing blood loss during surgery
  * there are two different types of diathermy devices, monopolar and bipolar, and both are different 
  * link I used https://teachmesurgery.com/skills/surgical-equipment/diathermy/ 
  * [Diathermy](https://electrorange.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Diathermy-Machines-1.jpg)
